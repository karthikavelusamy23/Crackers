## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end celebratory Indian luxury: vibrant, sun-drenched, radiant, and architectural. It captures the majesty of Indian festivals—Diwali galas, royal weddings, and grand heritage celebrations—through luminous daylight palettes rather than nocturnal backdrops. 

The aesthetic marries modern Indian minimalism with regal craftsmanship. High-contrast, razor-sharp architectural geometry establishes an uncompromised sense of precision and luxury. Every boundary is crisp, deliberate, and definitive.

Visual style attributes:
- **Luminous Radiance:** Light-drenched surfaces layered in warm ivory, sunlit creams, and soft celebratory pastels evoke silk brocades and marigold-strewn courtyards.
- **Architectural Crispness:** Structured, monolithic frames and gallery-style product showcases.
- **Opulent Modernity:** Vivid vermilion, royal marigold, and imperial antique gold accents punctuate pristine pastel planes, complemented by clean modern geometric typography.

## Colors

The palette is strictly radiant and warm. Dark and black surface canvases are strictly forbidden; depth is achieved solely through tonal pastel stepping and warm metallic boundaries.

### Surfaces & Backgrounds
- **Primary Canvas (Warm Ivory):** `#FFFDF9` — The universal light baseline, imparting an organic, hand-pressed cotton feel.
- **Pale Marigold Cream:** `#FFF7E6` — Used for secondary container sections, cards, and warm structural framing.
- **Delicate Blush Saffron:** `#FFF0EB` — Applied to hero promotional banners, sparkler showcases, and callouts.
- **Soft Pistachio Mint:** `#F0F8F2` — Utilized for eco-certified green fireworks, soundless displays, and status badges.
- **Pastel Festive Peach:** `#FEEFEA` — Accent wash for curated gift sets, celebratory bundles, and cart drawers.

### Accents & Metallics
- **Festive Crimson Vermilion (`#D9381E`):** Primary action color; conveys celebratory power, auspiciousness, and high urgency.
- **Royal Marigold Amber (`#D97706`):** Secondary accent; illuminates hover states, star ratings, and celebratory tags.
- **Antique Imperial Gold (`#C59B27`):** Fine detailing, borders, crests, badges, and luxury tier labeling.

### Typography & Structure
- **Deep Festive Umber (`#2D1810`):** Primary text color, providing near-black optical contrast with deep royal warmth.
- **Muted Walnut (`#594036`):** Secondary text, specifications, decibel ratings, and metadata.
- **Subtle Gold Border (`#EED9A4`):** Crisp dividing lines, structural grids, and container perimeters.

## Typography

Typography pairs two high-clarity geometric sans-serifs to avoid outdated decorative cliches and typewriter styles:

- **Display & Headings (Outfit):** Delivers clean architectural structure, balanced geometric curves, and contemporary luxury. All major display titles and category banners use uppercase or tight tracking to project confidence.
- **Body & Product Specifications (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for supreme legibility at small sizes, optimal tabular alignment for numbers, and modern warmth.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (Outfit):** Uppercase with generous letter-spacing (`0.08em` - `0.14em`), defining a regal, catalogued look for certifications (e.g., "GREEN CRACKER COMPLIANT", "AERIAL SHELL").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column rigid architectural grid inspired by modernist art catalog layouts:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 48px canvas margin, 24px gutter. Product grids use 3 or 4 columns with refined borders creating a modular frame matrix.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px canvas margin, 16px gutter.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, 20px canvas margin, 12px gutter. Grids collapse into full-width monolithic stacks or 2-column tight matrices with clean separators.

Consistent vertical rhythm relies on multi-tiered spacing: micro-details stay within `space-xs` to `space-sm` for compact luxury specification density, while editorial showcases expand cleanly using `space-xl` and section offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

To protect the pristine, light-drenched aesthetic, heavy dropshadows and dark blurs are prohibited. Depth is produced via tonal stacking and metallic framing:

- **Zero-Shadow Philosophy:** Elevation is communicated not through artificial shadows, but by layering pastels: `#FFFDF9` base surface yields to `#FFF7E6` (containers), which rests atop `#FFF0EB` (highlighted pods).
- **Imperial Hairlines:** Elements are bounded by crisp borders in Antique Imperial Gold (`#C59B27`) or warm metallic amber (`#EED9A4`).
- **Sunlit Highlight Hover:** Interactive surfaces do not cast shadows on hover; instead, their background transitions into a luminous tone (e.g., `#FFFDF9` shifts to `#FFF7E6`), accompanied by a color transition of the hairline border to Crimson Vermilion (`#D9381E`).
- **Modal Overlays:** Modal scrims use an ivory frost (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.85)` with a `12px` backdrop blur), keeping the atmosphere festive, bright, and daylight-rich.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- Border radius is applied consistently across buttons, inputs, modal dialogs, banners, chips, and image containers, using friendly yet refined curves (`0.5rem` base, with larger components scaling appropriately).
- Visual rhythm relies on geometric precision, balanced proportions, and clean styling reminiscent of high-end jewelry boxes and modern heritage architecture.
- Nested sub-elements mirror outer corner radii to maintain a cohesive, polished look.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#D9381E`, text `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid #D9381E`. Rounded corners (`0.5rem`). Typography: `label-lg` in uppercase. On hover: background shifts to `#B82810`, border color to `#B82810`.
- **Secondary CTA (Regal Outline):** Background `#FFFDF9`, text `#2D1810`, border `1px solid #C59B27`. Rounded corners (`0.5rem`). On hover: background fills with `#FFF7E6`, border transitions to `#D97706`.
- **Tertiary Link:** Inline Outfit `label-md` in `#D9381E` with a persistent `1px` underline offset by `4px`.

### Product Cards
- Built on `#FFFDF9` or `#FFF7E6` surface cards with a continuous `1px solid #EED9A4` frame.
- Rounded corners (`0.5rem`). High-resolution imagery sits neatly inside the padded frame or follows the card radius.
- Badges (e.g., "5000 WALA", "GREEN AERIAL CHRYSANTHEMUM") mount directly into the top-left corner as geometric ribbons in `#FFF0EB` with `#D9381E` text.
- Pricing displays use Outfit `headline-sm` in Deep Festive Umber (`#2D1810`), accompanied by strikethrough original prices in Muted Walnut (`#594036`).

### Badges & Chips
- Clean rectangles with soft rounded corners, padded at `4px 10px`.
- **Eco / Sound-safe Tags:** Background `#F0F8F2`, text `#1C592F`, border `1px solid #B8E2C4`.
- **Celebration Category Chips:** Background `#FFF7E6`, text `#2D1810`, border `1px solid #EED9A4`. Active state: background `#D9381E`, text `#FFFDF9`, border `#D9381E`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid #EED9A4`, rounded corners (`0.5rem`).
- Inner text uses Plus Jakarta Sans `body-md` in `#2D1810`.
- Focus state: border shifts to a razor-sharp `1px solid #D9381E` with zero glow/shadow outline.

### Lists & Specifications
- High-density firework specification tables (Shot count, Burst height, Decibel scale, Duration) feature alternating pastel stripes (`#FFFDF9` and `#FFF7E6`) bordered with hairline `#EED9A4`.
- Bullet points are replaced with miniature imperial diamond glyphs (`◆`) rendered in Antique Imperial Gold (`#C59B27`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Slightly rounded inputs framed in `1.5px solid #C59B27`.
- Selected state: solid fill with Festive Crimson Vermilion (`#D9381E`) featuring an interior geometric indicator in `#FFFDF9`.
