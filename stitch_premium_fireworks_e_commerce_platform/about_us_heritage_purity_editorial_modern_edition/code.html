The Alchemy of Light

Where Ancestral Firework Guilds Meet Contemporary Environmental Chemistry.

In Sivakasi’s arid, sun-baked landscape, pyrotechny is not mass manufacturing—it is an inherited sensory craft. For four decades, JANAKI&CO has guarded the delicate balance of grain density, hand-rolled kraft paper housings, and precision timing cords.

When national environmental directives called for reform, we didn’t retreat to substitutes; we partnered at the frontline with CSIR-NEERI to pioneer patented formulations. Our crackers generate 30–35% less particulate matter while actually deepening the rich golds, vibrant magentas, and thunderous resonance India expects.

The JANAKI&CO Promise “Every aerial burst and sparkler produced in our Sivakasi workshops is crafted fresh for the current season, eliminating aged duds and volatile warehouse deterioration.”

Chronicle of Excellence

From a Modest Shed in 1984 to India's Premier Fireworks Atelier.

Started four decades ago with four artisans hand-blending sulfur-free sparkler mixtures, JANAKI&CO today spans over 48 safety-zoned acres adhering to strict national defense and hazardous cargo protocols.
