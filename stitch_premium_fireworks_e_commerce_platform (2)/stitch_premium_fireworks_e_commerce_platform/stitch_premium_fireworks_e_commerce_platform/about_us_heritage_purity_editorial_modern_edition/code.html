The Alchemy of Light

Where Ancestral Firework Guilds Meet Contemporary Environmental Chemistry.

In Sivakasi’s arid, sun-baked landscape, pyrotechny is not mass manufacturing—it is an inherited sensory craft. For four decades, JANAKI&CO has guarded the delicate balance of grain density, hand-rolled kraft paper housings, and precision timing cords.

When national environmental directives called for reform, we didn’t retreat to substitutes; we partnered at the frontline with CSIR-NEERI to pioneer patented formulations. Our crackers generate 30–35% less particulate matter while actually deepening the rich golds, vibrant emeralds, and thunderous resonance India expects.

The JANAKI&CO Promise “Every aerial burst and sparkler produced in our Sivakasi workshops is crafted fresh for the current season, eliminating aged duds and volatile warehouse deterioration.”

Chronicle of Excellence

From a Modest Shed in 1984 to India's Premier Fireworks Atelier.

Started four decades ago with four artisans hand-blending sulfur-free sparkler mixtures, JANAKI&CO today spans over 48 safety-zoned acres adhering to strict national defense and hazardous cargo protocols.

1984 Genesis

Founding at Sivakasi Grounds

Pioneered low-smoke golden sparklers and long-lasting flower pots crafted specifically for South Indian wedding muhurats and temple deepams.

2002 Expansion

48-Acre Safety-Zoned Production Campus

Constructed isolated blast-proof sheds with natural earth embankments, earning the prestigious PESO compliance seal with zero reportable incidents.

2018 Green Revolution

CSIR-NEERI Green Transition

Became one of the first 10 certified manufacturers in India to completely convert 100% capacity to certified green crackers featuring SWAS and STAR formulas.

2025 Present Day

Direct-to-Patron Digital Logistics

Delivering fresh Sivakasi bursts directly to over 18,000 pin codes across India in waterproof 5-ply crates with full real-time explosive freight tracking.
